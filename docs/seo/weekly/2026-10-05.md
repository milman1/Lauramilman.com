What the numbers say:

- **Organic search is small and flat**: about 40 sessions a week from
  Google, Bing, Yahoo and DuckDuckGo combined (170 from Google in 28 days).
  That is the number the SEO work has to move.
- **Most sessions are probably not people.** 82% are desktop (5,015 vs 983
  mobile), unusual for jewelry, and dozens of collection pages each got
  35–75 "landings" in an even spread. That is a crawler pattern. Shopify
  doesn't filter bots the way GA4 does, so treat session totals as an upper
  bound until GA4 is connected.
- **ChatGPT already sends buyers.** 70 sessions and 1 order ($472) in 28
  days from chatgpt.com, more than Bing, Yahoo and DuckDuckGo combined (19). The
  GEO work (answer-first copy, FAQs, clear facts) feeds this channel.
- **Facebook: 526 sessions, 203 cart adds, 0 checkouts** in 28 days. A 39%
  add-to-cart rate with no checkouts is not normal shopper behavior. Check
  the ad's audience and placement and the Meta pixel, and test checkout on
  a phone.
- 70 sessions with no referrer all added to cart (70 of 70). Likely an
  internal test or a bot. Worth a look when GA4 is connected.
- **The Journal barely gets visits.** Articles drew 2–7 landing sessions
  each in 28 days; the blog index and tag pages (`/blogs/journal/tagged/…`)
  drew more than any article.
- Big orders (Aug 31 and Sep 7 weeks) have no referrer recorded, so their
  source is unknown.
