unsupported era or transient filter combination.

## AI-search and schema decisions

Google states that its usual SEO requirements apply to AI features and that
special AI files are not necessary for Google visibility. Maintain the existing
agent endpoints for their actual consumers, but spend effort first on crawlable
pages and reliable facts. [Google AI-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

FAQ rich results are generally limited to authoritative government and health
sites. LMNY should write useful answers for buyers without forecasting those
rich results. This corrects the September audit's rationale for FAQ markup.
[Google FAQ eligibility](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes).

Product structured data and Merchant Center feeds should agree with visible
price, availability, condition and images. Structured data helps eligibility;
it does not guarantee placement. [Google product data guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product).
