# September SEO goal — merchant draft

Status: draft, unconfirmed. Prepared by Astra on 2026-09-11 for issue #96.
This document proposes a goal; it does not record merchant approval or launch a
live content change.

Over the 90 days following approval, make Laura Milman New York easier to find
for pre-owned David Webb, Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels and Tiffany jewelry,
pre-owned watches, and lab-grown jewelry. Help shoppers evaluate the actual
pieces through clear condition, measurements, provenance where documented, and
accurate availability. Resolve the catalog and technical gaps in the September
9 audit while preserving the visibility of useful existing pages.

## Success measures

1. Non-brand organic discovery: record Search Console impressions and clicks
   by the six query families, excluding Laura Milman, Milman New York, Laura's
   Gems and spelling variants. Record organic sessions and purchases separately
   in the store's analytics/GA4; Search Console does not measure sessions.
   Compare the final 28 days with the first complete 28-day baseline and report
   absolute values plus percentage change, without combining incompatible
   metrics.
2. Indexed-page coverage: record indexed eligible product, collection and guide
   URL counts, submitted URL counts, and known exclusions at baseline and day
   90. Aim to reduce unintended exclusions and cover all priority landing pages;
   a larger raw URL count alone is not success, particularly for sold stock.
3. AI-search citations: a Luna worker runs the same 12 documented buyer prompts
   monthly, recording engine, date, wording, cited LMNY URL and whether the
   answer accurately represents that page. Compare observed citation counts
   with baseline. These samples are directional and do not represent total
   market share or guaranteed placement.

No Search Console or analytics export was available when this draft was
prepared. Traffic, conversion, ranking and citation baselines are unknown.
Numeric growth targets should be agreed after measurement is connected; none
are invented here. The 90-day clock starts on recorded approval, not on the
date this draft is committed.

Completion of the strategy document is separate from goal approval, later
implementation, deployment and the independent live SEO audit. See
`2026-09-plan.md` for scope and acceptance checks.
