## Watch listings

Every watch / timepiece listing (feed or boutique) follows the same formula:

- Spec: `lmny-feeds/docs/watch-listing-schema.md`
- SEO: `lmny-feeds/docs/seo-title-formulas.md`
- Jacob & Co. boutique: `lmny-feeds/docs/jacob-listing-formula.md`
- Cursor skill: `.cursor/skills/watch-listing-formula/SKILL.md`

Title: `{Pre-Owned|Unworn|New Vintage} {Brand} {Model} {reference}`.
Do not invent Pre-Owned or Unworn. Boutique Jacob condition is **New Vintage**.
Specs live in `custom.*` metafields, not an HTML spec table. No price in the body.
SEO description always ends with `Authenticated by Laura Milman New York.`
